How to Play the ABC QR Game 🎮

Step 1: Scan the QR Codes 📷
  Press "Scan Box Pertama" and point your camera at the first QR code.
  Press "Scan Box Kedua" and scan the second QR code to find its matching pair!

Step 2: See What Happens! ✨
  🎉 If they MATCH (WIN!):
  The game says "Tahniah!" and shows the correct letter card.
  You can click to watch a "Cara Menulis" video to learn how to write the letter (big and small!).

  😢 If they DO NOT match:
  The game says "Cuba lagi ya!".
  Don't worry! A special bonus button appears so you can sing along to a fun "ABCs Alphabet Song" video.

Step 3: Go Back Easily ⬅
  Finished watching your video? Just press the "⬅ Kembali" button.

Bonus Fun! 🎈
  At any time, you can press "Alphabet Match" to play interactive touch-screen games like popping balloons or chasing matching letters!
